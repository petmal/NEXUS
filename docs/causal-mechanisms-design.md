# Causal mechanism laboratory: implementation design

Approved scope: implement the researched architecture as an opt-in ontology while
preserving previous families, seed behavior, action contracts and receipts. Use the
trusted ontology registry; do not introduce installable player-supplied plugins.
No paid model evaluation, main merge or public MindTrial changes.

The first bounded implementation has three-valued controls x,y,context, a latent
material mode, an instrument correction, unknown input wiring and composed
transition/response operators. A specimen stores its complete applied preparation
history (at most two pulses); the hidden mode is reconstructed from this public
history and private immutable truth. This preserves history dependence without
putting hidden values into public specimen payloads, canonical hashes or events.
Every pulse records its settings at application time. Probe remains immutable.

Decision: prefer derived latent state to the proposed private state store.
Reason: the bounded history is a sufficient statistic already known to the player;
independent design review confirms no engine/session/replay changes are necessary.
Invalidates if future worlds have unbounded history or hidden exogenous events.

The full prior has 648 parameter/state/operator combinations and six jointly
graded transfer predictions. Worlds compose wiring, transition and response
primitives; supplied T1 laws, T2 fixed forms, T3 bounded structures and T0 resolved
predictions have separate public disclosures. D0 supplies sufficient experimental
records. Untreated T3 readings cannot guarantee identification before the first eligible
submission; rejection feedback can still permit an untreated-only eventual win.
Only supported cells are accepted; D4 remains unavailable unless a strict adaptive
versus fixed-batch advantage over the full legal domain is proved. Do not relabel
this family to fill unsupported axis combinations.

Supported live cells: T1/D1 (one untreated reading), T2/D2 (known forms, at least
three ternary readings for a guarantee), T3/D5 (construction), plus D0 at all T.
D2 publicly discloses response form 0 or 1; form 2 can retain ambiguity after every
untreated measurement and is therefore excluded from this cell. Every coefficient,
sensor-offset and initial-state combination under the disclosed form is admitted.
D2 uses a separate named response draw; other components retain same-seed pairing.
No hidden rejection sampling is used. D2 has an untreated-only public witness.

All 27 settings and histories of length 0..2 are legal, except the six explicitly
listed mission contexts cannot be measured. A reference subset of 568 experiments
is sufficient; it is a witness, not a restriction on legal experiments. Off-subset
measurements must update the exact posterior correctly. Identical fresh stock
replaces an exhausted specimen; no episode reset/replenish operation is introduced.

Core invariants: exact finite arithmetic, public bounded grammar, no private-state
or seed leakage, any operationally equivalent model accepted, failed finish is
recoverable and rules out a complete target, generic resource accounting, exact
replay, all diagnostic hints terminal-only. P0/P1 and R0/R1/R2 retain existing
scientific semantics; unsupported P values fail explicitly.

Independent arithmetic proof before production code: 648 worlds, 113 target
classes, 126 complete untreated-observation classes of which 120 remain target
ambiguous; a greedy public-information policy on 568 experiments resolves every
world in at most six readings and 37 reference experiment actions (before final
submission and logistics). The policy is not an optimality claim. Actual preloading plus P1 and final submission yields a
40-action guaranteed reference bound (R1 budget 48, R2 budget 40). Degenerate
worlds remain admitted. The construction certificate concerns identification
from untreated readings before the first eligible submission. It does not require
treatment for eventual victory or for every selected truth.

Inference uses immutable cached ternary partition masks and exact posterior
bitsets; observations and joint target exclusions can couple all parameters.
Avoid false factorization. Cache structural compilation, not selected private
answers. Log cold compilation/policy timings and terminal scientific diagnostics.

Expected remaining risk: no empirical claim that half of models fail until the
fresh-seed model pilot. History/cause discovery must be distinguished from API,
provider, arithmetic and resource failures.


Review refinement: untreated measurements alone do not guarantee identification
over the full prior before the first eligible submission. The reference selects preparation after earlier
readings, but no theorem requires that exact discovery→preparation sequence or
proves it cheaper than every fixed panel. The certificate states this scope;
D4 remains unsupported. This is an experimental family until real-model results
calibrate challenge. Its 648 worlds are fewer than some older families: the intended
challenge is joint hidden-state/structure reasoning, not raw enumeration size.

Compilation evaluates every finite mode/point pair once per law into immutable
tables (72 law combinations × 81 rows). Independent exhaustive one-step checking
proves any bounded history by induction; independent whole-protocol checks cover
all witness and mission outcomes. Joint partition masks, policy subproblems and
law tables have bounded caches. No CPU/GPU parallelism is needed at this scale.

On import, the causal world checks its reported budget, physical optimum, reference cost,
initial hypothesis count and family identity against the compiled world and public prior.
Qualification also recomputes the investigation certificate.
