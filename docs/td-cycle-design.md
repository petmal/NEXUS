# Bounded T/D cycle: system design

Implement the accepted 23 October roadmap through the live-evaluation boundary.
Preserve exact success, deterministic replay, standalone operation, and the current
recoverable baseline. A family feature must not leak into unrelated ontologies.

## Deliverables and boundaries

1. Extend existing private diagnostics with evidence witnesses and provenance.
   Nonterminal snapshots already exist and must remain read-only.
2. Qualify a twelve-condition revision screen, two repeats, and matched single-shot
   and public-readings consistency conditions. Generate inspectable MindTrial inputs.
3. Produce bounded operator-only selective-revision and two-module composition
   qualification artifacts. Do not advertise a production ontology or empirical
   difficulty before these checks and live evaluation establish it.
4. Package focused commits, machine-readable local validation, and a run handoff.
   No paid models, image publication, remote branches, or main changes in this step.

## Invariants

- Classification precedes finish feedback. Success never retrospectively supplies
  evidence. Empty beliefs fail closed.
- Witnesses contain public-history implications, not selected-truth explanations;
  detailed alternatives are operator-only and absent from player responses.
- Generic diagnostics consume an optional family-owned witness capability. Current
  families without that capability keep their existing semantics.
- The checker simulates only the submitted construction on disclosed archive and
  recorded reader observations; no hidden truth, enumeration or suggested repairs.
  Its calls are logged, consume no world action, and remain subject to harness caps.
- Single-shot changes only the first rejected physically eligible complete finish.
  Ineligible finishes do not reveal or eliminate a candidate. The policy is public.
- C, L*, C(L), and rival relationships are structural descriptors, not cognitive
  difficulty guarantees. Admission checks are role-specific and fail closed.
- Qualification distinguishes hindsight certificates, public policies and empirical
  outcomes. All new variants retain an independently replayed supported policy.
- Experiments retain source/configuration identities and all attempts. Provider stop
  reasons remain unknown to the service unless supplied by a harness artifact.
